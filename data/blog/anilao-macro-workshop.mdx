This April 2026, I took a ten-day macro photography workshop led by Mike Bartick, Dan Bishop and Max at Crystal Blue resort in Anilao Batangas.  In this workshop I learned so much but the one thing that I really got better at was using a snoot to photograph macro critters.  A snoot helps (what is it.) separate the subject from the background and really makes it pop.  At the end of the workshop, I got better at using the snoot to shoot all the macro subjects.  

<Image
  src="/static/images/anilao-macro-workshop-apr2026/sexyshrimp-snoot.jpg"
  width={800}
  height={840}
  alt="sexy sexyshrimp"
/>


The main topics that was covered in the workshop besides snooting included creating black and blue backgrounds, using bokeh, back lighting and composition.  

The key to achieving a black background is ensuring that there is nothing behind the subject.

<Image
  src="/static/images/anilao-macro-workshop-apr2026/goby-composition.jpg"
  width={800}
  height={840}
  alt="gobey"
/>

Using bokeh to separate the subject from the background is achieved by opening up your aperture.  For example, in the lionfish shot below, the background is not very interesting.  Using bokeh separates the background from the subject and really makes it pop.

Cozumel has always been on my top ten list of scuba diving spots. Since I live in Puerto Morelos, Cozumel is an easy ferry ride away ⛴️ 🚢 🛳️.

My dive buddy, Linda Elliott visited me in Puerto Morelos where we did some cenote dives which I will write about at some point and in the new year, we headed to Cozumel.

Cozumel is known for massive coral formations, variety of rays, turtles, nurse sharks, eels, and reef fish including angelfish, groupers, grunts, and more.

For this trip, I didn't have my usual Olympus OMD camera 📸 as it was acting up and I had to take it to the shop. I took this opportunity to dust off my Olympus TG6 which is a small but very powerful camera for shooting macro. Not only did I capture nice macro but I was also able to get some decent medium and wide angle shots like the pictures below.
